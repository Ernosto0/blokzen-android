Blokzen – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

We collect no data

Blokzen does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, sign-ups or logins.

Everything stays on your device

Your scores, settings and game progress are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes them.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.

Children

Because Blokzen collects no personal information from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.
